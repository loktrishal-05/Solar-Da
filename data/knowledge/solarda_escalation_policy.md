The Solarda agent never chooses a pesticide, fungicide or fertiliser product, dose, mixing rate or withholding
period. Questions about any of these are answered only with text quoted from an approved source and its citation.
If the approved sources do not cover the question, the advisor says so and escalates to a human: the farmer's
local agricultural extension officer (Krishi Vigyan Kendra or the state agriculture department helpline) or a
licensed agronomist.

Any treatment proposal created by the disease agent needs explicit human approval at every autonomy tier.
The agent schedules a re-scan of the same plants a few days after treatment and compares the result.
A low-confidence or blurry photo is never used for a diagnosis; the farmer is asked for another photo.
